Match the Tau viewer's ambient occlusion profile H2: GTAO fades horizon samples by their 3D distance (XeGTAO's smooth falloff, new `ao.falloffEndRatio` and `ao.falloffRange`) instead of cutting them at a depth slab, `distanceFalloff` defaults to 0.5, and the denoise no longer trims outliers. `ao.thicknessRatio` is removed; [BREAKING_CHANGES.md](https://github.com/taucad/nanoraster/blob/main/BREAKING_CHANGES.md) has the migration.
